Before you buy.

The details that matter when buying a takeoff bed or part from our inventory.

What is a new takeoff bed?

A new takeoff bed is a factory truck bed removed from a new truck, commonly during a conversion to a flatbed or service body. Check the individual listing for condition and included parts, and ask about any handling or storage marks.

What beds do you have, and what do they cost?

Our listed inventory includes: 2023–2026 Ford F-250 / F-350 short bed, white, new condition, $2,500, bed only. Call or text 214-524-8401 to confirm availability, exact fitment, condition, and what is included before purchasing or traveling. Ask about other takeoff parts in current stock.

What comes with a bed?

Check the “Includes” details on the individual listing. A bed-only listing should not be assumed to include a tailgate, lights, bumper, hardware, or other accessories. Confirm the exact contents of the item with Better Beds before purchasing.

How do I confirm a bed will fit my truck?

Send your truck year, make, model, bed length, and whether it has single rear wheels or dual rear wheels, plus the listing you are interested in. Better Beds will help check compatibility before you buy. Model year alone does not establish exact fitment.

Are the pictures of the actual inventory?

Photos on the Ford short-bed listing show the actual item. The sale is bed only; pictured accessories are not necessarily included. For listings still labeled “Photo coming soon,” the images are illustrations, not photographs of the actual item. Contact Better Beds to confirm condition and included parts before making a trip.

Do you still have older beds that can be repaired and painted?

Yes. A limited selection of older beds remains in our inventory and can be repaired and painted by arrangement. Contact Better Beds to discuss available beds, the agreed finish, price, and timing. Our main focus is new takeoff inventory. We are not advertising a general repair or bed-sourcing service.

Do you install beds or remove the old one?

Installation and removal are not offered. Customers arrange that work separately. Better Beds can use a forklift to load a purchased bed onto a suitable trailer or truck for transport; loading is not installation.

How does pickup work?

Pickup is at 3123 Cartwright Street, Dallas, Texas 75212. Call or text first to confirm the item, price, payment arrangements, and pickup time. Bring a suitable truck or trailer and tie-downs. Forklift loading is available for purchased beds.

Can an out-of-state buyer purchase a bed?

Out-of-state buyers can arrange pickup in Dallas. Customer-arranged freight may be possible; confirm loading and carrier requirements with Better Beds before booking transport. Do not assume shipping or delivery is included.

Will you find a bed that is not in your inventory?

Better Beds now focuses on selling the inventory we have on hand. Contact us to check current stock. A bed not listed or confirmed by Better Beds should not be assumed available, and we are not advertising a bed-finding service.

How do I measure my bed?

Measure inside the bed from the front wall of the box to the inside of the closed tailgate. Send that measurement with your truck year, make, model, and rear wheel setup so we can check the listing with you.
