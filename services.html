Repair & paint for
our in-stock beds.

Giving the beds we own another useful life.

Alongside new takeoffs, we prepare selected older beds from our own stock for sale. Depending on the bed, that can include body repairs, surface preparation and paint.

Only beds we own and have in stock.

We are not accepting customer-owned beds or trucks for repair or paint. Work, price and timing are confirmed for each bed. Installation and removal are not offered.

A bed-by-bed process

From our stock to ready for pickup.

  1. 01

    Start with one of our beds

    Tell us about your truck and the bed you’re considering from our in-stock inventory. We’ll check fitment and discuss its condition.

  2. 02

    Agree on the work

    We assess what the bed needs and confirm the scope, finish, price and expected timing with you before work begins.

  3. 03

    Prepare, repair & paint

    Work may include body repairs, sanding, priming and paint, as agreed for that bed. Finish and color options depend on the project.

  4. 04

    Review, then arrange pickup

    Confirm the finished condition, included parts and pickup details with us. Wait until we confirm the bed is ready before traveling.

Timing is agreed individually. There is no standard turnaround promise. Ask us about current work availability for the specific bed before making plans.
